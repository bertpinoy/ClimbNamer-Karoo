ClimbNamer 1.0: names for the climbs on your Karoo route, tested on real rides.

- "Climb name" data field: distance to the next climb, "Climbing X/Y" with distance to the top and grade, and "No more climbs" after the last one.
- Alert with the climb's name at the start or 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 km before it.
- Climb names as summit labels on the map.
- Names are looked up as soon as a route is loaded (built-in Benelux hill list, Nominatim, Overpass), over Wi-Fi or the companion app, and stored on the Karoo for offline use.
- Navigating to a route from home: the climbs on the route are detected from its elevation profile.
- Control Center progress, activity log with export.
