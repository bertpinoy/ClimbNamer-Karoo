Much faster climb names, also when you load the route as you leave home. Climbs that end at a named hill or pass in Belgium, the Netherlands or Luxembourg are named instantly from a built-in list (no connection needed). Other climbs are looked up via Nominatim first (a few seconds per climb, also over the companion app); Overpass, which was often slow or busy, is now only the backup. The Activity log shows which source named each climb and how long it took.
