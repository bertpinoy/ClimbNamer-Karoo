Fixes from the first real ride. The climb list is now fixed when the route is loaded, so climbs keep their numbers (climb 2 no longer becomes climb 1), and your position comes from GPS matched to the route, so the distance updates on a climb, alerts before a climb work, and the data field shows "Climbing", "Next climb in X km" and "All climbs finished" after the last climb. Failed name lookups keep retrying while the route is active (every 10 minutes after the first tries), with a "Retry now" button and the reason per climb in the app. Overpass is now queried with GET, which suits the companion-app connection. New "Activity" section in the app shows what happened during a ride.
