Fixes: climbs were sometimes placed at the wrong spot on the route (mirrored from the finish), which gave them the names of other roads. Climbs are now always placed from the start of the route, and the elevation profile is only used when it looks valid. The alert duration offers 5, 8 or 15 s (the fourth option was cut off). The app's "Current route" list now shows where each climb starts and how long it is, plus diagnostics. Names stored by 0.1.0 are looked up again.
