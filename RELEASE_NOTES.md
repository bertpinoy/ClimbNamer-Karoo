Navigating to a route from home (or anywhere away from it): ClimbNamer now detects the climbs on the route part you'll ride from the route's elevation profile, because the Karoo only lists the climbs on the way to the route until you get there. All climbs are known and named as soon as you load the route. The detection is tuned against Hammerhead's own climb detection on real routes. The diagnostics line shows how many climbs came from the Karoo and how many were detected.
